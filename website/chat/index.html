How it works

Enter a name and you're visible while you're here. Text whoever is online, and send Tomato a hello whenever its bridge is up — a real computer answers from its screen. If hardware Tomato is offline, notes queue automatically and deliver when its bridge is back — or preview any draft in Virtual Tomato from the chat box (labeled virtual, never sent to hardware). Leave, and your chats are gone for good.
