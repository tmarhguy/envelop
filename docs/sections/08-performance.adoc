[#performance]
== Performance and limits

No latency or throughput measurements are claimed in current sources;
the numbers below are design bounds, not benchmarks.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Bound | Value | Notes
| Device text | 1–256 printable ASCII bytes | Longer text is `TBD (unmeasured)`
| Wire payload | At most 512 bytes | Frame at most 522 bytes with header and CRC
| BLE chunk | At most 20 bytes per RX write | Nordic UART transport capacity
| Route table | At most 8 contacts | Session-local; cleared on `CONTACT_RESET`
| Bridge lease | 30 seconds, renewed while healthy | Fencing mechanism, not a health proof
| End-to-end latency | `TBD (unmeasured)` | No measured send-to-ACK figures in source
| Sustained throughput | `TBD (unmeasured)` | No measured jobs-per-minute figures in source
|===

Timeout, lease loss, disconnect, or an ambiguous claimed job is an
unknown hardware outcome. It must stay visible as unknown rather than
being converted into a virtual result or a performance claim.
